# Rights-holder concerns, takedown and contact

LDA Local Graph · Last updated: 2026-10-16.

We respect the intellectual property of Optimizely and everyone else. If you believe this project infringes your rights, breaches an agreement or is misleading, please tell us. We will act in good faith and promptly.

## Contact
- Email: legal@codingkoala.io
- Post: Coding Koalas Pty Ltd, Sydney NSW, Australia
- Publisher: Coding Koalas Pty Ltd (ACN 668 775 433)
- Please do not use public GitHub issues for legal notices.

## What to include
1. Who you are and the right or interest you represent.
2. The specific file, line, name or behaviour concerned (link or path).
3. Why you believe it is a problem.
4. What you would like us to do.
5. How to reach you.

## Our commitments
| Step | Target time |
|---|---|
| Acknowledge receipt | 2 business days (Sydney time) |
| Initial response and, where the concern is credible, temporarily disabling or removing the material | 5 business days |
| Full response or proposed resolution | 15 business days |

- If a concern is about trademarks or naming, we will generally change names first and discuss afterwards.
- If a concern is about code or content, we will review it against our clean-room record (CLEAN-ROOM.md) and share what we can.
- We will not hold a request against the sender, and we are happy to talk informally first.
- Nothing in this policy is an admission of liability or waives any right.

## Security issues
If a security problem could affect a real site (for example a production site wrongly pointed at one of these local services), email security@codingkoala.io with "SECURITY" in the subject. We aim to respond within 2 business days.
